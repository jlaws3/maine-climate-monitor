Source: Copernicus ERA5-Land daily mean 2-meter air temperature; baseline 1961–1990.

Methodology: Temperature anomaly is the selected daily mean minus the average for that calendar day during 1961–1990. Statewide and county statistics are weighted by the geodesic land-area overlap between ERA5-Land grid cells and county polygons. Map shading uses inverse-distance interpolation between original ERA5-Land grid-center coordinates, clipped to the same county polygons used to draw boundaries. Interpolation is for visualization only and does not increase the underlying data resolution. ERA5-Land is reanalysis data, not direct weather-station observations. Geography: Plotly US county boundaries. Optional basemap: OpenFreeMap. Drought polygons and county coverage are derived from the weekly U.S. Drought Monitor. D0 is abnormally dry, not drought. Both datasets show their actual observation dates. Prototype visualization.
