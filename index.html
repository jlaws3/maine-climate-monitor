Source: Copernicus ERA5-Land daily mean 2-meter air temperature. Historical baseline: 1961–1990.

Methodology: Temperature anomaly is the October 15, 2025 daily mean minus the average of October 15 daily means during 1961–1990. Statewide and county statistics are weighted by the geodesic land-area overlap between ERA5-Land grid cells and county polygons. Map shading uses inverse-distance interpolation between original ERA5-Land grid-center coordinates, clipped to the same county polygons used to draw boundaries. Interpolation is for visualization only and does not increase the underlying data resolution. ERA5-Land is reanalysis data, not direct weather-station observations. Geography: Plotly US county boundaries. Optional basemap: OpenFreeMap. Prototype visualization.
